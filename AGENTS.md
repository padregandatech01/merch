# Loxada department portal

- For any analytics or reporting work, follow the [Loxada OLAP reporting standard](https://github.com/hilocarloarmenion03/admin/blob/main/loxada/olap-reporting.md) and the [department BI guide](docs/bi-standard.md). Keep operational forms separate from reports.
- For supplier application decisions, only an exact pending submission can be reviewed. Preserve the submission revision check and the read-only record for saved decisions.
